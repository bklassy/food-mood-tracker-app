Cozy-style Android / Kotlin food and mental health/wellness tracking app.
The food tracking uses intuitive eating principles, so no calorie tracking.
With the app you can track meals with the hunger/fullness scale, energy, and nervous system each part of the day:
Twilight, Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening, Late Evening.

Also tracks social interactions, activity/exercise with reflection, and bowel movements ¯\_(ツ)_/¯
Swipe left to track your favourite foods, and swipe right to track favourite exercises and mental health tools.

This app is not meant to optimise your life with tons of data, it's meant to help reconnect a little to your body, hunger cues, and nervous system.

That said, if you do want to analyse for trends, you can export your tracking with the export-to-csv feature (two swipes to the left)
